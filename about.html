About the System

Understanding our AI-based traffic prediction prototype.

🚦 AI Traffic Prediction System

The AI Traffic Prediction System is a prototype designed to predict traffic congestion levels and recommend better routes to users.

The system focuses on traffic conditions in selected areas such as Kakinada, Rajahmundry, Visakhapatnam and surrounding locations.

How It Works

The system follows a simple process to analyze traffic and recommend routes.

📍

1. Select Location

The user enters the starting location and destination.

🤖

2. Predict Traffic

The AI/ML system analyzes traffic patterns and predicts congestion.

🛣️

3. Recommend Route

Available routes are compared and a suitable route is recommended.
